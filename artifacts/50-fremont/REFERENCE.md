## Orientation

Yaw −44.9° (OSM bearing 134.9°). +Y = true north. Faces: **NE = Fremont St** (long
entrance face; cloud and trees), **SE = Mission St**, NW toward Market, SW toward First.
The tower is on the west side of Fremont (45 Fremont is ≈ 62 m across the street axis).

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Pale masonry tower with a dense punched-window grid.
2. Serrated corner bays stepping back into a setback crown.
3. Blue Salesforce cloud on the facade.

## Preserved

- 183 m crest, OSM footprint and heading; three setbacks; notched corners all the way up.

## Simplified

- 43 floors of square windows → punched pane grid at 4.1 m storeys, 3.4 m bays.
- The vertical glass strips in the stepped corner bays and the mid-height louvred band
  → omitted; corner notches carry the serration.
- Crown → dark louvred box with a pale cap and a white glow line.

## Uncertainties

- Setback heights are estimated; the cloud's face (Fremont) is inferred.
- The draft placed the cloud and trees on the SW (First St) side; corrected to Fremont.
