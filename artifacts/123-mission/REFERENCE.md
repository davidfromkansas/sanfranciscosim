## Verified dimensions and location

- Anchor `-122.3944470, 37.7918240`.
- Modelled plan 40.5 x 33.5 m, 3.2 m notches.
- Shaft roof 114 m (≈ LiDAR majority 112.6), crown to 120 m, crest **124.0 m** (published).

## Orientation

True-world, +Y north. Local +X = bearing 134.5°. **Mission = NW face** (34 m, trees).

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Pale cream stone prism with fine dark grid.
2. Notched corners.
3. Set-back crown storey.
4. Tall colonnaded base.

## Preserved / simplified

- Preserved: window density (2.6 m pitch), corner notches, base piers.
- Simplified: OSM's SE jog dropped; louvred mezzanine folded into the base.
- All materials on-palette.

## Uncertainties

- Whether 124 m is crown top (assumed) or main roof.
- 28 vs 29 storeys.
