## Orientation

Yaw +45° (local +x bearing 45°, along Market). +Y = true north. NW = Market St
(colonnade), NE = Spear St, SW = Main St, SE = toward Mission / 60 Spear.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Horizontal striping: broad travertine bands over dark bronze ribbon glass.
2. The Market colonnade with a planter box per bay on its roof.
3. A long, low, flat-topped slab among towers.

## Preserved

- Slab roof and crest heights, OSM extent and heading, the colonnade + planters.

## Simplified

- Paired spandrels and the thin teal reveal line → one stone band per storey.
- Colonnade bays → 12 square piers; planters → box + shrub mass.

## Uncertainties

- The rear block height (23.9 m) and its skin are **estimated/inferred** — not in the
  photo, and LiDAR's majority only resolves the slab.
- The Spear St end is assumed to match the Main St end.
- Architect (SOM) comes from batch research, not from the fetched Wikipedia extract.
