## Verified dimensions and location

- Anchor `-122.3957540, 37.7917820`.
- Modelled plan 56 x 41 m with 3 m corner notches.
- Main roof 145 m (LiDAR plane), crown wall crest **150.0 m** (published 149.96 m).

## Orientation

True-world, +Y north. Local +X = bearing 44.9° (along Mission). **Mission = SE face**
(long), **Beale = SW face** (short). Trees on Mission.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Pale grey granite slab.
2. Dense vertical piers with tiny punched windows.
3. Slotted crown.
4. Blank notched corners.
5. Lobby colonnade.

## Preserved / simplified

- Preserved: the 2.9 m pier rhythm (one pier per window bay), the crown slots, the notches.
- Simplified: the notches are symmetric (OSM's are ~5 x 1.6 m and slightly uneven);
  the entrance stair and canopy are omitted.
- Off-palette: `Toy_concrete` for the granite.

## Uncertainties

- NW/NE faces not photographed; inferred identical.
- Possible demolition under the 2025 Hines proposal.
