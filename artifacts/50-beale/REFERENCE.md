## Verified dimensions and location

- Anchor `-122.3964860, 37.7912570`.
- Modelled plan 70 x 38 m.
- Main roof 93 m, penthouse to 96.6 m, crest **100.0 m** (published).

## Orientation

True-world, +Y north. Local +X = bearing 134.8° (along Beale). **Beale = NE face** (long,
trees), Mission end = SE, Market end = NW.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Darkest box on the block — bronze glass, black grid.
2. Long slab proportion.
3. Regular grid, no setbacks.
4. Colonnade base and street trees.

## Preserved / simplified

- Preserved: proportion, colour, grid regularity, the recessed base.
- Simplified: mullion rhythm doubled to 3.3 m fins; the plaza is a plain plinth.
- Off-palette: `Toy_bronze`, documented.

## Uncertainties

- Penthouse inset/height inferred from LiDAR spread.
- Rear faces inferred identical.
