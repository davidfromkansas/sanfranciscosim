## Orientation — the terrace corners

Faces at 45/135/225/315°; local +X runs along Howard (45°). Howard frontage = SE face,
Beale = SW face.

The photo is taken from across Howard (the former temporary-terminal block, bus
canopies in the foreground) looking north-west: the wide Howard face is square-on and
the Beale face is foreshortened on the left. The stacked open terraces are cut into
the **right-hand corner of the Howard face = the true EAST corner**, from about two
thirds up to the roof, and the left face's far edge steps back at the same height =
the **true WEST corner**. Wikipedia's "NW and SE corners" is the street-grid naming
(Howard taken as east–west), i.e. the same two true corners. The draft modelled one
notch on the true NORTH corner; it is now two notches, east and west.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. The two opposite corner terrace stacks in the upper third.
2. The crisp blue-glass prism with a fine grid.
3. The glass crown screen over the slab above the roof.
4. The low glazed podium with a planted edge.

## Preserved

- 183 m crest, 168 m roof, OSM heading, two terrace stacks (one every two floors,
  planted, with a lamp for night), crown screen on the notched plan.

## Simplified

- Terraces as slab + one tree + a lamp each; railings and furniture omitted.
- Storeys grouped in 7.8 m bands; fins every 3 m.
- Podium as one glazed box with trim columns.

## Uncertainties

- Whether the west corner carries terraces or is a plain setback (photo shows only
  its edge); modelled as terraces per Wikipedia.
- The stack start height (~116 m) is read from a perspective photo.
- Crest: 183 m (batch) vs 184.4 m (605 ft).
