## Verified dimensions and location

- Anchor `-122.4007860, 37.7900167`.
- Modelled on the true 5-node OSM wedge (no simplification).
- **Height 36.6 m** — published (Wikipedia/Emporis roof 36.58 m / 120 ft); LiDAR roof 38.1 m majority.

## Orientation

True footprint used directly (`tk.ccw`). **Market = SE face** (two collinear edges, 26 m), **Sutter = N face** (21 m), the **prow faces E** (6.5 m) at the Market x Sutter point; the W edge is a party wall.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. The wedge plan and narrow prow.
2. Deep cornice over arched top windows.
3. Cream terracotta piers.
4. Prow bay stack.

## Preserved / simplified

- Preserved: wedge plan, prow bays, arched top floor, cornice depth (exaggerated).
- Simplified: brackets, ornament and the oriel subdivisions omitted.

## Uncertainties

- Published 36.6 m vs LiDAR roof 38.1 m: the model follows the published roof.
- Sutter-side detail inferred to match Market.
