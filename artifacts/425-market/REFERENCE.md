## Orientation

Yaw −44.9°. +Y = true north. Faces: **NW = Market St** (short face), **NE = Fremont St**
(long face, street trees), SW toward First St, SE toward Mission. The tower holds the
SW corner of Market × Fremont.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Long, wide, flat-topped blue-grey slab.
2. Crisp silver aluminium grid over deep-set glass.
3. Colonnaded base set back from the sidewalk.

## Preserved

- 160 m crest, OSM footprint and heading, the grid rhythm (storey bands + 3 m fins).

## Simplified

- Deep window reveals → thin proud steel bands and fins on flat glass.
- Top → a recessed louvred penthouse with plant (the photo shows a flush solid band at
  the very top; the recess reads better from the aerial camera).

## Uncertainties

- Published 160 m sits 2.3 m above the LiDAR max; the penthouse fills the gap.
- SW and SE faces not photographed; assumed identical.
- The draft put the trees on the SW (First St) side; moved to Fremont.
