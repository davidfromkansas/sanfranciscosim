# 575 Market Street — reference dossier

575 Market Street (Market Center, mid-block between First and Second), San Francisco. Hertzka & Knowles, 1975; Standard Oil Building No. 4, later Chevron Tower.
Plan: `docs/asset-plans/575-market-street.md`. Labels: **published**, **measured** (OSM / DataSF LiDAR),
**inferred** (read from a photo or the description), **estimated** (a modelling choice).

## Orientation

Local +x = bearing 135.1° (away from Market). NW narrow end = Market St; the long faces face NE (toward First) and SW (the shared plaza and 555 Market).

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Tall pale slab with a tight grid of tall dark windows.
2. End-on to Market.
3. Plain solid top band.

## Simplified

- Window grid coarsened to fit 5,000 tris (4,916).
- Night lights in two-storey pairs.

## Uncertainties

- The photo is at dusk: the wall reads grey-beige; published 'terra cotta' could be warmer.
- Window bay ~3.3 m is coarser than the real grid (triangle budget).
