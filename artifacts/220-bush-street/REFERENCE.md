## Verified dimensions and location

- Anchor `-122.4013027, 37.7914191`.
- Modelled plan 42.6 x 13.5 m (OBB less the cornice overhang).
- **Height 92 m** — published (Wikipedia infobox roof 92 m); LiDAR roof 93.1 m majority.

## Orientation

Local +X = bearing 171° (south). **Bush St = S face (13.5 m)**; the long E face looks toward Sansome over lower buildings; the long W face is against the Mills Building up to 47 m; N face toward Pine.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Thin buff slab rising behind the Mills Building.
2. Vertical window strips.
3. Arched top storeys and cornice.
4. Stepped penthouse.

## Preserved / simplified

- Preserved: slab proportions, strips, arched attic, blank party wall below 47 m.
- Simplified: Romanesque ornament omitted; window strips continuous with spandrel bands.

## Uncertainties

- Only a distant photo of the tower; E/N facade detail inferred.
- Penthouse form simplified.
