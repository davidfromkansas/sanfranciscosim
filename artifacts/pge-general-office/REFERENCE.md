## Verified dimensions and location

- Anchor `-122.3968490, 37.7923850` (OSM OBB centre).
- Modelled plan 43 x 41 m.
- **Crest 79.8 m, measured** (LiDAR max 79.77 m, 2010). Rooftop plant reaches it; the
  set-back top block's cornice is at 78 m.

## Orientation

True-world, +Y north. Local +X = bearing 135.3°. **Market = NW face (local −X)**, Beale
Street = SW, Matson = NE, annex = SE. Street trees modelled on the Market side.

## Recognition cues (ranked)

1. Cream terracotta block matched to Matson.
2. Row of tall arched windows under a heavy cornice.
3. Stepped top: balustraded attic, then smaller top block.
4. Arcaded base.

## Preserved / simplified

- Preserved: the order of the crown (arches → cornice → attic → set-back top), the
  arcaded base, the punched shaft.
- Simplified: urns, pilasters and the Market entrance arch are omitted; the top four
  storeys' "multiple setbacks" are reduced to two tiers; all faces share one treatment.
- Off-palette: `Toy_terracotta`, documented.

## Uncertainties

- CTBUH 93.5 m vs LiDAR 79.8 m (possible post-2010 rooftop addition).
- Setback depths estimated from one photo.
- Rear/side elevations inferred.
